Authorship

A typographic voice for an agentic system for science.

Ultra Sans was designed and authored by Amil Khan, a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

The typeface was created for Ultra, an agentic system for science. Its geometry is tuned for quick interface scanning, sustained reading, and precise scientific data.

Ultra Sans is derived from DM Sans and incorporates adapted Inter geometry. The original designers and project authors remain credited; the Ultra Sans authorship covers the derivative design, programmatic construction, and typographic direction.

The UCSB affiliation identifies the author and does not imply that Ultra Sans is an official University typeface or that the University endorses the project.
