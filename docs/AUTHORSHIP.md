# Ultra Sans authorship

## Author

**Ultra Sans was designed and authored by Amil Khan**, a PhD student in the
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of
California, Santa Barbara.

Khan created the typeface for **Ultra**, an agentic system for science. Ultra
Sans gives that system a consistent typographic voice across scientific
interfaces, long-form reading, and data-dense displays. His contribution
includes the design direction, geometric studies, programmatic construction,
OpenType feature work, and visual evaluation of the derivative family.

- Author: [Amil Khan](https://www.amilworks.io)
- Affiliation: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of
  California, Santa Barbara
- Project: Ultra, an agentic system for science
- Source: [github.com/amilworks/ultra-sans](https://github.com/amilworks/ultra-sans)

## Design lineage

Ultra Sans is a derivative work. It begins with DM Sans and incorporates
adapted geometry from Inter. The original DM Sans designers, copyright holders,
and Inter Project Authors retain credit for their respective work. Authorship of
Ultra Sans refers to Khan's design and engineering of the derivative family; it
does not replace or diminish those upstream credits. The complete source and
license record is maintained in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

## Affiliation statement

The UCSB affiliation identifies the author. Ultra Sans is not an official
University of California or UCSB typeface, and the affiliation does not imply
University endorsement of the font or the Ultra project.
